Changelog

Every version of Bubba Machine. The format follows Keep a Changelog and versions follow semantic versioning: while the version starts with 0, any minor version may change how it plays or the pattern format.

[0.9.0-beta] - 2026-10-18

HOME is the console. Ten patterns, six channels, mutes, scenes and CUE on the keys, like an MX-1; REC plays and records any channel without leaving the console; each machine keeps its own GLO. Beta: not fully tried on the device yet.

0.9 has only 10 patterns: on first boot patterns 11-16 are dropped, and the song and the scenes start empty (their format changed). Patterns 01-10, settings, takes and your sounds are kept.

Added

  • The console (HOME › MIX): white 1-10 = patterns 01-10 (hold one + tap another = chain), white 11-16 = channels T1-T6 (tap = cursor), black 1-6 = mutes (hold = solo), black 7-10 = scenes A-D, black 11 = CUE.
  • Channel card: hold a channel's white key on the console. Let go = enter the machine. While you hold it, ALGORITHM changes the machine (T3-T6, leave it 2 s on one; BUNO-6 and BITTAL one at a time) and PRESETS its sound; turning a knob means letting go stays on the console.
  • CUE: hold black 11 (or tap it to keep it on) and the mutes you tap drop together on the next bar.
  • SLOOP-REC: REC on the console arms the cursor channel and the keys play it without entering: notes chromatic from F (bass machines an octave down, KEYS IN SCALE respected), drums and pads on the white keys with black 1 = accent. It records with the expressive REC (FREE REC on an empty channel) and draws the channel as a piano roll. Hold REC = only while held.
  • PIANO ROLL view (machine GLO › VIEW): the whole loop of a note channel with pitch rows fitted to its notes, a keyboard that flashes with what plays and the notes recorded this pass in red. Same keys as the normal view.
  • FX › CHANNEL on the console: per-channel DRIVE (soft-clip), PAN, DUCK and BEAT FX on/off (whether the BEAT FX and the punch-ins touch that channel). Saved with the pattern.
  • Console GLO: TEMPO (BPM, SWING, MASTER LENGTH, TRANSPOSE, AUTO FILL), MACHINES (the machine of T3-T6), SCALE (ROOT, SCALE, KEYS IN SCALE, BASE CHORD) and SONG.
  • HOME + white 11-16 from any screen: go to that machine when you let go of HOME.
  • Historic factory sounds (92 in all): the TB-303 owner's manual sounds and acid classics on BB-303 (12), Minimoog sound charts and recreations on B-MINI (18), DX7 recipes on BX-7 (20), Juno factory patches as starting points on BUNO-6 (16) and PPG-style wavetables on BITTAL (26), plus sounds inspired by famous records. Names only allude: no artist names or trademarks. Sources in assets/presets/SOURCES.md.
  • BITTAL ENV2 POS (FILTER + page): envelope 2 sweeps the wavetable position, as on a PPG.
  • py -3 tools/fm1/consola.py estado shows the power rails, how each boot ended (power on, brownout, long press, watchdog) and where the firmware was.

Changed

  • Scenes are mutes only (channel mutes and BR-8 / BP-1 instrument mutes), kept with the project, not with a pattern. Tap an empty one = store; tap = launch on the next bar; hold 2 s = store over it (a ring fills); EDIT held + scene = clear; hold one + tap others = chain. Live mutes stay when the pattern changes.
  • Song entries are a pattern plus an optional scene; SONG REC records pattern changes and scene launches.
  • HOME tap always goes to MIX. HOME's pages are MIX, FX (CHANNEL › DELAY › REVERB › MASTER) and GLO. On MIX, PRESETS changes the cursor channel's sound; on FX and GLO it is MOVE. HOME held + K1-K4 = the cursor's mix strip.
  • Machine GLO replaces MORE: GLO inside a machine opens its list (VIEW, LENGTH, SPEED, TRANSPOSE, ACCENT, MIDI OUT, INIT…); GLO again goes back. Machines are changed from the console (the card or GLO › MACHINES); the machine is still saved with the pattern.
  • OCT−/+ always change the octave on note machines, also in SEQ and in SLOOP (they do nothing on drums and pads). Step pages moved to black 9 / 10 (SEQ and the ACCENT, REPEAT / TIE, ERASE and FILL screens); the LENGTH layer uses black 11 = +16; ERASE has UNDO / REDO on black 6 / 7.
  • Drums and pads: black 11 = other section (HI/LO); hold it = instrument MUTES while held.
  • Questions: keep holding the control that asked for 2 s (a bar fills) to say yes; letting go, or any other control, is no. OCT+ no longer answers yes. Copy track (HOME + EDIT) pastes with EDIT held 2 s.
  • BB-303 rebuilt on Open303 (MIT): the TB-303 filter, the accent circuit, the amp envelope and declicker, and the knob ranges of the TB-303. 12 sounds.
  • BUNO-6 about 15 % lighter (the Juno-60 high-pass once on the channel sum, a 12 dB low-pass before the chorus) and BITTAL about 25 % lighter. Voices share one memory pool: the FM-1 system has 151 KiB free instead of 121.
  • The kick cuts through: the factory 808, 909 and SYNTH kicks were revoiced (about +6 dB over the rest of the mix in their first 30 ms) and the duck holds 30 ms before it releases.
  • Lighter screen: curves are computed once per change, big redraws are spread over three frames, the display is sent in the background and capped at 25 fps.

Fixed

  • The FM-1 switched off during fast navigation with TWO CORES (0.8): it was a brownout. The firmware now sets the stock power rails at boot, logs the exact reset cause and keeps the hardware watchdog on.
  • TWO CORES safety: if the second core hangs, its channels go silent and it retires until power-off; a TWO CORES boot that did not reach 5 s turns TWO CORES off on the next boot.
  • The duck did nothing on a BUNO-6 with chorus, and kicks played live from the keys did not trigger it.
  • An effect that broke (NaN) went silent for good; it is now reset on the spot.
  • BR-8 hits stop at −70 dB (was −90): hats and long tails were still being computed when you could no longer hear them.

Removed

  • Patterns 11-16 (10 patterns now).
  • The MACHINE page inside a channel and MORE (now the machine GLO).
  • FILL on HOME (black 11 is CUE). On drums and pads the FILL screen is gone: use AUTO FILL (GLO › TEMPO), FILL + ROLL or the FILL ONLY / NO FILL step conditions. Note channels keep their FILL screen on black 11.
  • Scenes with a pattern, HOME's TEMPO, SCENES and SONG pages (now in GLO), HOME + SELECT with its box (use HOME + white 11-16), HOME + K1-K4 for BPM, swing, master length and drive (now in GLO › TEMPO and FX › MASTER), and OCT+ = yes.

[0.8.0-beta] - 2026-10-16

Choose your instrument. The drums and the pad sampler stay fixed; T3-T6 pick their machine, and every machine is laid out like the real one.

0.8 changes the pattern format (and the B-MINI / BUNO-6 sound format): on first boot it erases the saved patterns. BITTAL sounds and DX7 banks still load.

Added

  • MACHINE page on T3-T6: BB-303, B-MINI, BX-7, BITTAL or BUNO-6. Turn ALGORITHM to one and leave it 2 s to load it (built for live use: no question). Notes, loop, ARP, scale and mix stay; the machine is saved with the pattern.
  • Heavy machines one at a time: BUNO-6 and BITTAL can only be on one track; elsewhere they show "ON T6" and are refused with a message. Also enforced when copying a track or loading a pattern.
  • BX-7 LFO page (SPEED, DELAY, PMD, AMD) on top of the patch, and a BB-303 sound bank (8 sounds).
  • py -3 tools/build.py longrun runs minutes of simulated playing to catch audio that degrades over time.

Changed

  • Every machine's SOUND pages follow its real panel, in order and with its names: TB-303 (VCO VCF, ENV), Minimoog (CONTROL, OSC 1-3, MIXER, FILTER, F/L CONTOUR, OUTPUT), DX7 (ALGORITHM, EG, VOICE, LFO), Vital (OSC, FILTER, ENV, LFO, MACROS) and Juno-6 (LFO, DCO, HPF VCF, VCF MOD, VCA, ENV, CHORUS).
  • K1-K4 on SEQ are each machine's four most-played controls: BB-303 CUTOFF · RESO · ENV MOD · DECAY; B-MINI CUTOFF · EMPHASIS · CONTOUR · F DECAY; BX-7 MOD LVL · FEEDBACK · DECAY · RELEASE; BUNO-6 FREQ · RES · ENV · LFO.
  • ALGORITHM on BB-303 = waveform, on B-MINI = MONO / LEGATO. Voices are shared by machine, not by track (a BX-7 on T3 is polyphonic).
  • The voice brake no longer silences voices again and again under load: it lowers a voice cap (never below 4), fades release tails first and gives the voices back when the load drops.

Fixed

  • The BUNO-6 chorus got rough after about 6 minutes (a sample counter lost precision); it now stays clean.
  • Voices went silent after a while under load (the brake kept fading the newest notes).
  • Notes were lost when a stolen voice ended during its 2 ms fade.
  • BB-303 and BX-7 showed envelope knobs that did nothing.

[0.7.0-beta] - 2026-10-15

One machine per screen. HOME becomes the BX-1 machine with its own pages, and every screen loses the text it did not need. Design: the "Bubba Machine UI 0.7" canvas.

Added

  • MOVE (PRESETS on HOME): one knob that moves the whole mix. Left: the master low-pass closes and the mix goes into the echo. Right: the master high-pass opens and the mix washes into the reverb. Live, not saved.
  • A DJ filter on every track (K2 on HOME): left low-pass, right high-pass, open in the middle. It uses a reserved byte of the track, so saved patterns keep working.
  • HOME's pages: MIX › FX › TEMPO › SCENES › SONG, shown as a strip at the top. The FX page has a third sub-page, MASTER: master filter, BEAT FX and drive.

Changed

  • HOME's K1-K4: level, filter, delay and reverb of the cursor track (before: volume, reverb send, master filter, BEAT FX).
  • Tempo has its own color (yellow) in the header and in its box, which shows four beat blocks. Turning ALGORITHM no longer lights K1's dial.
  • Inside a machine: "<" before its name (HOME goes back), and the page name with one dot per page instead of the row of tabs.
  • Less text: the hint lines show for the first 4 seconds of a page and then go away; the bottom hint line, the CPU readout ("CPU!" only when the audio does not keep up), HOME's column headers and its master bar are gone.
  • FADERS left HOME's cycle: the MIX page already has every level.

Changed (UX review: every machine is its own instrument)

  • The lights speak one language on every screen and on keys and buttons alike: off = does nothing, dim = available or silenced, medium = has something and will play, on = selected or a hit, blink = playhead or needs you, breath = armed. See "The lights" in the manual.
  • Drum MUTES screen: only instruments with steps are lit; playing ones flash on every hit, muted ones are dim, empty ones are off. The same live flash on HOME's track keys, the pads and the notes a track plays.
  • Buttons that do nothing are off. With HOME held, only the buttons with a HOME + function light up; inside a layer, only the ones that work in it. SAVE breathes with unsaved changes (on the console), HOME blinks with a pattern or scene in the queue, PLAY marks the first beat of the bar, REC tells armed, capturing and overdub apart.
  • HOME inside a machine always goes back to the console; a global screen opened from a machine (GLO, HOME + FX, SAVE, TIME, SCALE, SONG, SYSTEM) goes back to that machine.
  • ROLL on BR-8 and BP-1 (ARP): a tap keeps the roll screen open. Rates 1/8 to 1/32 with triplets, CRESC / DECRESC, swing, a tail after release and FILL + ROLL on black 11; with REC the roll is written as repeats and accents.
  • The big knobs are labelled (SEL · ALG · PRE under the dials, "-" when they do nothing) and have jobs where they were dead (FX page, SAVE).
  • Knob feel: acceleration comes from how fast you turn, lists move one item per click, on/off settings are right = on and left = off, and MOVE, the DJ filter and PAN stop at the center. SELECT goes through sub-pages (PADS, ENV, MIX › DUCK) before the next tab.
  • Same setting, same knob: GLO held = BPM, SWING, transpose, octave; BX-7 and BB-303 SOUND keep SEQ's K1-K3 with ACCENT on K4.
  • Screen: the line that says how to exit stays; the header shows the six tracks' mutes and hits, the queued pattern as A01>A05 with a beat countdown, SONG and a green autosave dot (no more AUTOSAVED box); the 2 s ring names what it opens; short drum names (BD, SD, CH, OH…) that don't collide; K1-K4 values on the top line instead of a box over the grid; the small font gained > < , ! ? ' and a readable #.

Fixed

  • Holding REC to record could erase the track (for example while recording the mix on the sampler). REC held 2 s is now a deliberate live erase: instant, no question, undoable with HOME + OCT−, the ring says ERASE T#, and it never fires while anything is recording or on the RECORD and TAKES pages.
  • LFO no longer opens an empty page on BR-8, BP-1, BB-303 and BX-7: a tap keeps its layer open (repeat on the grids, tie on the notes).
  • Holding a step and turning PRESETS replaced the whole kit or patch with no undo; it now sets the step's condition (drums) or length/TIE (notes). Changing a kit with PRESETS can be undone.
  • HOME on the recorder threw away an unsaved take; it now asks.
  • The FILL screen made OCT− breathe and the MUTES screen made SEQ breathe; the page light could land on the wrong button (ENV on drums lit EDIT).
  • Releasing a chord key on a scale-lock page cancelled a +7 / +9 that was still held. PRESETS in the CHORDS and ARP layers changed the sound; PRESETS on the project SCALE page read past a table.
  • The SYSTEM LED setting turned dim keys off too (it now only affects buttons) and MEDIUM did nothing.
  • The FM-1 restarted on BP-1's PADS page. The SDK's snprintf does not understand %+d (nor %f): it skipped the argument, the next ones shifted, and the pad line's last %s read a number as a pointer (an invalid bus read). All of Bubba's code now uses its own snprintf (core/bm_printf.c), checked against the C library's on the computer. The 30 %+d on screen also showed wrong text before.
  • Processor faults leave a trace: a copy of the SDK's exception handler (hal/fm1/sdk/wl82_debug.c) stores where it happened, the task and what was being drawn before restarting, and the console log shows it on the next boot.

[0.6.3-beta] - not released on its own (included in 0.7.0-beta)

Two cores that really work in parallel. Measured on the FM-1 with a six-machine pattern.

Changed

  • TWO CORES: each core has its own software interrupt (IRQ_SOFT2 on core 0, IRQ_SOFT3 on core 1, raised through the shared latch the SDK uses for task switches). A job always goes to the core that is not running the audio task, which the SDK scheduler moves between cores; before, half the jobs ran in series on the audio core itself.
  • Automatic split: every few blocks each machine's cost and core 0's fixed work (sequencer, mix) are measured, and the split of the six machines that leaves both cores most even is chosen (only if it improves by 20 %). Any machine can run on either core; on the computer the output is identical sample by sample.
  • Voice brake acts only when a block is about to overrun (95 %) or the sustained average passes 85 %; in 0.6.2 it switched off about one voice per second and ate the BUNO-6 pad.

Added

  • External clock warnings: changing CLOCK shows a message, PLAY with an external clock and no pulses says "EXT CLOCK: WAITING FOR MIDI", and the header's "EXT?" turns red. Twice on the device CLOCK had been switched to EXTERNAL by accident and PLAY seemed frozen.
  • Lab test 36: pick the core split by hand (or automatic) to compare.
  • Console: the split, the estimated load of each core and the jobs that ran in series.

Fixed

  • Lab tests in safe mode reset the chip: they left the watchdog on, and nothing feeds it in safe mode.
  • The second core's longest wait showed 71 s (a clock read from the other core wrapped around), and the same wrap could fire the timeout and switch TWO CORES off.

[0.6.2-beta] - 2026-10-15

First version with data from the device. With 0.6.1 and a pattern using all six machines, audio did not keep up on the FM-1 (88-115 % load, dropouts), and TWO CORES made it worse (123 %). Beta: in the first test on the device, the same kind of pattern stayed around 50 %.

Added

  • Voice brake: if an audio block uses more than 85 % of its time, the quietest voice of the pool (T2, T4-T6) fades out (2 ms). Better to lose one voice than to cut everything. The console counts how many it switched off.
  • Lab test 35 (py -3 tools/fm1/traza.py una 35): how long the second core takes to answer and whether it respects the floating point of the task it interrupts.
  • Console: the voice brake, the second core's state and its longest wait.

Changed

  • TWO CORES is off by default. The second core now computes T4-T6 inside a software interrupt registered on it, without going through the system scheduler; before, it was a task that took turns with the audio one. The first time it is turned on it is checked to answer; if it does not answer or takes more than 4 ms, it goes on with one core until the next boot.

Fixed

  • The lab tests run again: since 0.6.1 they asked for ~75 KiB of heap and 58 were left. Now each test uses only its own buffers.
  • The FM engine test from safe mode reset the chip (the FM tables do not exist until Bubba starts). Now it warns and does not run.

[0.6.1-beta] - 2026-10-14

Do not install 0.6.0-beta: it left 38 KiB of RAM for the FM-1 system, which needs about 50 KiB at boot, so it most likely did not start properly. 0.6.1-beta leaves 126 KiB and the installer no longer offers 0.6.0. Beta, not yet tested on the device.

Added

  • Control platform (control.html): with the FM-1 connected over USB, it shows the memory (free RAM, audio load, takes, the shared zone and the language) with warnings based on what you are doing, uploads and deletes takes (the audio is converted in the browser), loads and deletes sounds (Vital, .syx, .bsnap) and loads or removes the language pack. Without the FM-1 it does nothing.
  • Console, command 23: the memory and library report (takes and user sounds) and deleting a take; command 22 deletes a user sound by its number.
  • RAM check when building the firmware: build.py fm1 fails if the system heap drops below 120 KiB. The computer tests could not see it.

Changed

  • RAM: the lab buffers (~59 KiB) are taken from the heap only while a test runs, and the delay drops to 0.80 s (was 1.49 s; a 1/4 delay gets shorter below 75 BPM). Fixed RAM goes from 472 to 384 KiB.
  • The sampler has a 6 s cap for all takes; the rest of the 100 KiB zone is for sounds.
  • TWO CORES is on by default so it can be tested on the device. A hang or safe mode switches it off.
  • The language pack is managed on the control platform: the installer no longer loads it.

[0.6.0-beta] - 2026-10-14

Beta, not yet tested on the device: it passes the automated tests and the emulator. If something fails on the FM-1, go back to 0.5.0-beta or to the factory firmware from the installer.

Six fixed machines instead of engines to pick, a new face and a sound bank. The pattern format changes (format 7): saved patterns are erased on first boot (takes are kept).

Added

  • Six machines, one per track, named after the ones that inspired them: BR-8 (T1, modeled drums), BP-1 (T2, pad sampler), BB-303 or B-MINI (T3, switched with ALGORITHM, notes stay), BX-7 (T4, FM), BITTAL (T5, wavetable) and BUNO-6 (T6, polysynth). On HOME, the BX-1 mixer.
  • B-MINI: three oscillators (TRI, SHARK, SAW, SQUARE, WIDE, NARROW) in 32' to 2' ranges, noise, ladder filter with drive, CONTOUR and LOUDNESS, LFO and glide.
  • BITTAL: two wavetable oscillators (up to 8 frames, built from a 256-harmonic spectrum, alias-free on every note), up to 4-voice unison, LP 24/12, BP, HP or ladder filter, two ADSRs, LFO and 4 macros with targets. The oscillator pages draw the table in 2.5D. ALGORITHM picks oscillator 1's table from the bank.
  • BUNO-6: 6 voices with one oscillator each (saw, pulse with PWM, sub and noise), 4-position high-pass, 24 dB filter, one ADSR, delayed LFO and stereo chorus I, II or I+II (ALGORITHM).
  • Sound bank (.bsnap) for B-MINI, BITTAL and BUNO-6: 37 factory sounds. BITTAL's and B-MINI's come from freely licensed Vital presets (CC0, Unlicense and GPL-3.0), renamed and credited in CREDITS.md; BUNO-6's 12 are original, made in Vital. tools/gen_snaps.mjs builds them with the source commits pinned.
  • Your own sounds: the web editor (sonidos.html) turns a Vital preset into a Bubba Machine sound and sends it to the FM-1 over WebMIDI; it also loads DX7 .syx banks (32 voices) into BX-7. On the computer: tools/vital2bsnap.mjs and tools/fm1/sonido.py. In the emulator, the Sound button.
  • BX-7: the algorithm drawn as on a DX7 (carriers, modulators, feedback and each operator's level) and PRESETS with the voices of your banks.
  • Reverb (Dattorro) with a per-track send in MIX and its page in HOME + FX; master filter (low-pass to the left, high-pass to the right) and BEAT FX (a punch-in that stays on) on HOME.
  • Expressive REC on note tracks: each note keeps its real micro-timing and gate, is cut at the bar if still held, and whatever you turn on K1-K4 while holding it stays as a P-Lock. With REC, SELECT picks a step, ALGORITHM its gate (up to TIE) and PRESETS its micro-timing.
  • Long note with two white keys: hold the step where it starts and tap the white key where it ends.
  • BP-1 sustain loop (the pad's LOOP page): held, it repeats a section; let go and it plays on to the end.
  • Per-track MIDI OUT (MORE): the track plays and also sends its notes over MIDI. Replaces the MIDI engine.
  • Audio load (CPU) in the header.
  • Two tests in SYSTEM, off by default: FAST SCREEN (SPI at 30 MHz: a frame drops from 85 to 39 ms) and TWO CORES (T4-T6 on the second core; on the computer it gives the same samples). If the FM-1 hangs or boots into safe mode, both switch themselves off.
  • Plug and play web installer: the page downloads the ready-made Bubba Machine package (no M-VAVE V15 needed), installs it and, if the page is in Spanish, loads the language pack at the end. The V15 is only needed to go back to the factory firmware.

Changed

  • The new face: header, viewport, keyboard and dials always in the same place, with a neon palette (cyan active, amber values, red REC and mutes, mint long notes). On SEQ the white keys are the steps; the K1-K4 dials show value and name.
  • The knobs: on HOME, SELECT is the mixer cursor, ALGORITHM the BPM and K1-K4 volume, FX send, filter and BEAT FX. Inside a track, ALGORITHM is the machine's mode (instrument, pad, algorithm, table, chorus). HOME + SELECT changes track. The 400 ms box and AUTO ENTER are gone.
  • Takes share 100 KiB of flash with the bank sounds (takes from the bottom, sounds from the top). Up to 3 s per take.
  • Gone: ANALOG, free tracks, KIT on T4-T6 and the MIDI engine: each track is its machine.
  • 22 voices in total, with a per-track cap.
  • Deterministic audio: it sounds the same on every boot.

[0.5.0-beta] - 2026-10-12

Beta, not yet tried on the device: it passes the automated tests and the emulator. If something goes wrong on the FM-1, go back to 0.4.0-beta or the stock firmware from the installer.

The screen switches to English, so Bubba Machine can be understood worldwide. Spanish is still available as a language pack, not loaded by default. Take memory goes down from 7.4 s to 6.4 s: 16 KiB of flash now belong to the language pack, and a take that used that space is lost. The sampler becomes a pad sampler and the pattern format changes: saved patterns are erased at the first boot (takes stay).

Added

  • Language packs. The screen's phrases are in English and a pack (.bmlang) translates them. It lives in its own flash zone (0xD9000, 16 KiB), so it does not touch patterns or takes and a firmware update does not erase it. SYSTEM › LANGUAGE switches between English and the loaded pack. Whatever the pack does not translate shows in English.
    • Spanish (lang/es.txt, built into lang/es.bmlang): the screen looks as before.
    • It is loaded on the FM-1 with tools/fm1/idioma.py (over the USB-MIDI console, with the transport stopped) and in the emulator with a button; on the Spanish emulator page it starts in Spanish the first time.
    • tools/gen_lang.py collects every phrase into lang/plantilla.txt, checks each translation (ASCII only, the same %) and builds the packs. Anyone can make another language.

Changed

  • T2 is a 16-pad sampler, like a KO II or the drum sampler of an OP-1 field. It replaces the sliced loop with a generator (and SLICER, CORTAR and CHOPS).
    • Each pad is a piece of a take, with its own start and length (they can overlap), pitch, level, key mode (ONESHOT, KEY, THRU: held, it keeps going through the take, LOOP), direction, attack, release, choke group and whether it follows the tempo. A kit can mix takes.
    • The take builds the kit: when a take is saved with the kit empty, it is cut on the hits, each cut goes to a pad and the grid is written so it plays like the take. In TAKES: N1 kit on the hits, N2 in 16, N3 in 8, N4 the whole take on the selected pad; white keys play it in 16 parts.
    • SEQ is a grid like the drums: one line per pad, with its length, accents, repeats, conditions, mutes, fill and per-step P-Lock of FILTER, PITCH, DECAY and START. No more loose numbers: you see which pad plays on which step.
    • PADS (EDIT): the take's waveform with the pad in brackets and the other pads marked, and three pages (TRIM, SOUND, ENV; EDIT again changes page). Black keys: copy, paste, empty, start on the hit; 9-11 reverse, half speed and stutter.
    • BREAK (SEQ again): the old generator, now on the grid and off by default. VARY changes pads, ANCHOR keeps the strong beats, REPEAT and BREAK. The PLAYS row shows what is going to play and EDIT + black 4 writes it into the grid.
    • On the recorder, white keys play the new take before it is saved.
  • The sampler drawing (SOUND) is the same mirrored waveform; the three frequency lanes are gone.
  • Drum model names, FM patches and waveforms are in English too (translated in the pack).

0.4.0-beta - 2026-10-11

Beta: the first version that runs on the FM-1. It was tested on a single device (the author's) and still changes a lot. An update may erase what you saved (patterns, song, settings and sampler takes): until v1 there is no backward compatibility, and when a format changes the old data is erased at boot and the demo comes back. If something matters to you, record it from the audio output before updating.

The sounds were rebuilt: no more recorded sounds or wavetables, everything is computed live with proven synthesis libraries. What 0.3 saved (patterns, song, settings and takes) is not read: it is erased on first boot and the demo starts.

Added

  • Modeled drums (T1 and KIT tracks): 16 instruments, each with its own model: 808 kick, snare and hat plus 909-style synthetic versions (the Plaits models by Mutable Instruments), metallic hat and cymbals, clap, tom and conga, cowbell, resonator (rim, clave, block), noise (maracas) and FM percussion. Kits 808, 909 and SINTE. Each instrument is edited in INSTR: TUNE, DECAY, TONE, COLOR (whatever fits the model), MODEL, LEVEL, choke group and LENGTH. Accents hit differently, not just louder. Six drum voices (was four).
  • New ANALOG, a real virtual analog: two variable-shape polyBLEP oscillators (SAW, SQUARE with pulse width, TRIANGLE, folded SINE and 3 SAWS), sync, ring, sub and noise; LP 24/12, BP or HP filter with resonance and drive; filter and amp ADSR; LFO to pitch, cutoff, shape or level; POLY, MONO or LEGATO with glide. 16 presets.
  • Paged sound editor (SOUND for ANALOG, INSTR for drums): 4 settings per page on K1-K4, SELECT changes page, EDIT/ENV/LFO jump to the right one, with a drawing of what each page does (wave, filter curve, envelope, LFO).
  • 6-operator FM engine (msfa, the base of Dexed and of the factory firmware) for T3 and the free channels: 16 original patches (E.PIANO, FM BASS, BELL, BRASS, STRINGS, ORGAN, CLAVI, MARIMBA...), with the BRILLO (brightness), REALIM (feedback), DECAY and ALGORITMO (all 32 DX7 algorithms) macros and a screen showing the 6 operators. MORE > ENGINE > FM.
  • Sampler: holding a step plays the loop from there, whole and looping, while you hold it.
  • Free recording on note tracks, like a looper: on an empty track, REC has no fixed length; when you close it, the track gets whole bars (1-4) and every note keeps how long you held it.
  • Length in bars on the SEQ layer (black 7-10).
  • New sound engines: ACID (303-style: 4-pole ladder filter with resonance, accent and slide) for T3 and the free tracks; ANALOG (two detuned oscillators, sub and noise, 6 timbres) for the free tracks; and the SINTE kit, drums computed live. The recorded KO-808 and KO-909 kits stay.
  • Take memory in view: on the recorder (free seconds and KB, maximum per take) and in the emulator, next to audio loading.
  • SLICER (SEQ again on T2): the sampler cuts the take into 16 slices, one per white key. White keys play the slices and, with REC and PLAY, set them on the step; K1 picks, K2 moves, K3 sensitivity, K4 gain; black 1-3: slices on the hits, 16 or 8 equal; 9-11 reverse, half speed and stutter. Slicing on the hits looks for the attacks (it also works for vocals).
  • Key strip on screen: every track page, MEZCLA (mix) and ESCENAS (scenes) draws the 11 black and 16 white keys with a short name and the same state as their light (the lights are white and don't say what a key does). On HOME the strip is large. Where the white keys are already on screen (SEQ, CORTAR, SLICER), the strip shows only the black keys.
  • Drums in sections of 8: the drum SEQ shows 8 instruments with their names in regular type; black 9 switches section.
  • Upload a take from the computer: tools/fm1/toma.py converts any audio (with ffmpeg) and sends it to the FM-1 over USB-MIDI; it arrives as a freshly recorded take.
  • New demo: acid techno at 160 BPM (909, a 303 line with accents and slides and the sampler slicing the first take).
  • FM-1, full Bubba Machine for the device (beta, tested on the author's FM-1): the core with sound through the codec (ALNK0), matrix and LEDs on interrupts, screen, USB-MIDI, saving, safe mode and the update service.
  • FM-1, diagnostic firmware: no sound that shows every button, key and knob, MASTER, the battery and USB-MIDI, with safe mode, a way into chip mode and the update service.
  • Web installer (instalar.html): builds the package in the browser on top of M-VAVE's FM-1 V15 (which each user downloads) and installs it with the official loader; it reads the FM-1 by itself (says which firmware it runs, or why it can't find it), you choose what to install (Bubba Machine, diagnostic or factory firmware) and press one button; it also goes back to the factory firmware and resumes an interrupted install. tools/fm1/servir.py serves it on the computer with V15 already loaded.
  • FM-1 tools: tools/fm1/fwsc.py (packer, checked byte for byte against V15) and tools/fm1/rescate.py (rescue from Windows through chip mode, with a full backup before writing).

Changed

  • Black 10 and 11 on tracks open screens. 10 (drums) opens the instrument MUTES and 11 opens the FILL screen, where the fill plays and the white keys build its hits (or mark notes as fill-only), with K1 = automatic fill. A tap keeps them open (close with the same key or HOME); holding = only while held. A tap no longer mutes blindly: every mute shows a message and a muted instrument turns red.
  • ALGORITHM inside a track also goes through the box with the number before switching; in INSTR it picks an instrument and plays it.
  • HOME explains the black keys (mutes, solo, scenes, fill and the drum screens).
  • Old engines removed: BASS, SYNTH (wavetable), MONO and the 6-timbre ANALOG become the new ANALOG; the recorded KO-808/KO-909 kits and the SINTE kit become the modeled drums. The FM-1 program is 148 KiB smaller and 64 KiB of RAM are freed.
  • Patterns in format 5: each track stores 32 sound settings, each drum instrument its model and hit, and each sampler note its slice (4 bits). Takes move to the BMT4 signature (16 slices in the header).
  • Factory loops removed: the sampler plays only takes (recorded or uploaded with tools/fm1/toma.py).
  • ANALOG on the FM-1: 4.1 % of a core per voice (own polyBLEP oscillator, filter coefficients every 8 samples, division-free saturation and short per-block passes; the first version cost 10 %).
  • Audio code is compiled for speed: on the FM-1 the demo with PLAY went from 36.9 % to 27.7 % of a core. Wavetables are built at boot from their spectrum (56 KiB less program).
  • 44.1 kHz audio end to end, with a floating-point bus. The engines used to run at 22 kHz and the output was doubled; they now run at 44.1 kHz (highs up to 20 kHz, no oversampling images) and the mix, delay, effects and clipper are floating point, block based. The delay and the effects capture (stutter, reverse, tape stop) are now 16-bit (the capture used to be 8-bit μ-law), and sampler recording goes through a Butterworth filter before dropping to 16 kHz. On the FM-1, with PLAY, the mix costs less than before even though it computes twice the samples (docs/TRAZA_FM1.md).
  • DROP interpolation (no interpolation) becomes LINEAR: at 44.1 kHz, repeating samples sounded lo-fi.
  • The SEQ page of note tracks is only about that track: a piano roll of the page with each note's length, the track's bars and the dotted tempo follower. It no longer shows the other tracks.
  • Recorded notes can last up to 4 bars (before, 1).
  • Flash map for the device: global settings move to 0xDD000 and the take library becomes 116 KiB (7.4 s in total; before 8.1 s), to make room for the waiting loader and the SDK store.

0.3.0 - 2026-10-10

Interface 3.0, complete in the emulator. It does not read 0.2 patterns, settings or samples: on first boot it erases them and starts with the demo. The FM-1 port is still untested on hardware.

Added

  • Four gestures per button that mean the same thing on every button: tap (its page; again, the next one), hold (its live layer, with the keyboard drawn on screen), hold 2 s and HOME + button (its global version).
  • HOME everywhere: home screen with the pattern bank, a controller when held on any screen (white keys = queue a pattern, two whites = chain, black keys = mute or solo, scenes and FILL) and a modifier: undo and redo, back to step 1, SONG REC, song, global delay, project scale, save as and copy a track onto another.
  • ALGORITHM navigates: on HOME, a box with the target track and entry after 400 ms (0, 250, 400, 800 ms or OFF); inside a track it switches and keeps the page; with HOME held it picks a track from any screen.
  • Tabs per track that SELECT walks, and the MAS (more) tab with everything about the track.
  • 3 fixed and 3 free tracks: T4-T6 pick the SYNTH, MONO, KIT (a second TR drum machine) or MIDI engine (sends its notes over MIDI).
  • New layers: SEQ length and speed, ENV paint accents, LFO paint repeats or ties, SAVE copy the pattern (or one track) somewhere else, GLO transpose the whole project with TAP tempo on PLAY.
  • Sampler 3.0: a loop in 8 slices that a generator rearranges bar by bar, with the TU row (silence, generator or pinned slice) and the SUENA row (what it picked), the VARIAR, ANCLAR, REPETIR and ROMPER knobs (vary, anchor, repeat, break), detected kick and snare (K and S), loop B with its chance, weighted retrigs, live reverse, half speed and stutter, and CAPTURAR (capture) the variation that is playing.
  • The loop follows the tempo and the screen says how many semitones it moved; PITCH corrects it and MAS > USAR BPM DEL LOOP sets the project to its tempo.
  • A dedicated recorder (REC on the sampler): armed to the bar, with the seconds left in large digits, an unsaved take that already plays and is saved on stop.
  • Take library of free-length takes (8.1 s in total, up to 3 s per take) with BPM, bars and gain, and the CORTAR (slice) editor: cuts on eighth notes, on the hits or even.
  • Three-level accent on notes (normal, accent, ghost) and how much accent per track.
  • A scale per track; the project scale lives on HOME + SCL.
  • Questions before erasing (OCT+ yes, HOME no).
  • Website and documentation in English too, with a language switch on every page (emulator included).

Changed

  • GLO is no longer the modifier: HOME is. GLO opens the global list and, held, the TONO (pitch) layer.
  • REC acts on release: tap = record, hold = record while held, 2 s = clear the track (asks).
  • A track's black keys follow a single grammar: 1-8 the elements, 9 and 10 modify, 11 FILL or STUTTER.
  • The sampler went from 16 chops to 8 slices, and from 2 fixed 4 s slots to the take library.
  • The sampler can also duck under the kick's sidechain, and the demo lowered its levels so it does not clip with the break playing.
  • Format 3 for patterns, settings and takes.

Removed

  • The 0.2 SAMPLER screen (now SONIDO, TOMAS, GRABAR and CORTAR) and slots A and B.

0.2.0 - 2026-10-10

First public version. Runs on PC and in the web emulator; the FM-1 port has not been tested on hardware yet.

Added

  • TR drums (T1) and sampler (T2) with 16 instruments, each with its own line and length (polymeter), accent, ×2-×4 repeats, flam, P-Locks and steps that only play (or stay silent) in the fill. Live roll and TR-style erase.
  • Per-track time: length 1-64 and speed ×2 to ×1/4; master length from 16 steps to infinite.
  • Sampler: records the mix with live chops; 2 slots of 4 s in flash, played directly (XIP).
  • Factory loops in ROM (BUBBA 160 and BUBBA 128), synthesized by tools/gen_loops.py. The demo starts with one.
  • Sample drawing by frequency: three lanes (lows, mids and highs) instead of the waveform, which is a solid block with heavily compressed music.
  • Per-track note looper on the synths (REC › PLAY › OVERDUB).
  • Chord grid 4×4 with degree, name and function by color; progression sets, voice leading and strum.
  • Scenes A-D with chains and SONG REC; automatic fills.
  • Third press of 2 s (TIME, SYSTEM, SAMPLER, clear track, quick save) with a progress ring.
  • GPL-3.0-or-later license, documentation, website and CI.

Changed

  • The project is released as Bubba Machine, independent from any earlier firmware.
