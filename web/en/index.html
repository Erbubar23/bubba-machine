Free firmware for your M-VAVE FM-1

Turns the FM-1 into a live groovebox with six machines: drums, a pad sampler, a bass, FM, a wavetable and a polysynth with chorus, each with its own length and its own speed. Free code, licensed under the GPL-3.0.

What it does

Six machines to play live

Everything is designed around the FM-1 controls: three big knobs, four small ones, fourteen buttons and 27 keys with LEDs.

T1

BR-8

Modeled drums (808, 909 and SYNTH), computed live. Accent, repeats, flam, per-step P-Locks and steps that only play in the fill.

T2

BP-1

16 pads made from your takes, as on a KO II or an OP-1 field, with sustain loop and BREAK to vary the grid bar by bar.

T3

BB-303 · B-MINI

Acid bass with slide and accent, or three oscillators into a ladder filter. One knob switches them and the notes stay.

T4-T6

BX-7 · BITTAL · BUNO-6

6-operator FM with .syx banks, a wavetable that loads Vital presets and a polysynth with chorus. 37 factory sounds, plus yours.

HOME

BX-1 mixer

Volume, reverb send, master filter and BEAT FX. Four gestures per button; holding HOME is the controller on any screen.

LIVE

Scenes and effects

Expressive REC, four scenes with chains and song recording, automatic fills, reverb, delay and 16 punch-in effects.

How to contribute Report a problem
  • Original code, written from scratch. Only hardware facts from public FM-1 reverse engineering were used. Credits.
  • Sounds computed live. The firmware ships no third-party recorded audio; the community presets in the bank are freely licensed and credited.
  • Always with its source. Any version that is distributed, modified ones included, has to publish its code.
  • Tested. Over 600 automated checks run on every change.
