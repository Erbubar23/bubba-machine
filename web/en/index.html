Free firmware for your M-VAVE FM-1

Turns the FM-1 into a six-track live groovebox: TR drums, a sampler, a bass and three synths, each with its own length and its own speed. Original code and sounds, licensed under the GPL-3.0.

What it does

Six tracks to play live

Everything is designed around the FM-1 controls: three big knobs, four small ones, fourteen buttons and 27 keys with LEDs.

T1

TR drums

Two synthesized kits. Accent, repeats, flam, per-step P-Locks and steps that only play in the fill. Roll and erase while playing.

T2

Sampler

Records the mix, marks chops live and sequences them like the drums. Comes with two factory loops.

T3

Bass

Monophonic wavetable with filter and glide, plus an unquantized note looper.

T4-T6

Three synths

Polyphonic wavetables with arpeggiator, scales and a chord grid that shows what goes with what.

TIME

Every track at its own pace

Length from 1 to 64 steps and speed from ×2 to ×1/4 per track. A 12-step hi-hat against a 16-step kick.

LIVE

Scenes and effects

Four scenes with chains and song recording, automatic fills and 16 punch-in effects.

How to contribute Report a problem
  • Original code, written from scratch. Only hardware facts from public FM-1 reverse engineering were used. Credits.
  • Original sounds. Kits, waves and factory loops are synthesized in the repository: the firmware ships no third-party audio.
  • Always with its source. Any version that is distributed, modified ones included, has to publish its code.
  • Tested. Over 300 automated checks run on every change.
