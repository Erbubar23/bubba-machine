Authorship

Bubba Machine is a new project by Erbubar23, released under GPL-3.0-or-later. The project's own code, screen fonts and logo are original; third-party libraries and presets are listed below with their license.

Hardware knowledge

  • Felucca (hugelton/Felucca, GPL-3.0-only), by Leo Kuroshita and Hügelton Instruments. Its public reverse engineering documented the FM-1 hardware. Bubba Machine does not use its code: only hardware facts (pins, timings, memory map), cited with their source in docs/SPEC_V3.md §11.
  • JieLi AC79 SDK (Apache-2.0): register reference for the port. When the port uses SDK binaries (bootloader, configuration), they will be distributed under their Apache-2.0 license, which is compatible with GPL-3.0.

Factory sounds

BR-8 kits are original to the project. Many factory sounds recreate historic machines from published settings (the TB-303 owner's manual, Minimoog sound charts, DX7 recipes, PPG manual tables): no audio was sampled and no factory data was copied; each source is listed in assets/presets/SOURCES.md. Several BUNO-6 sounds start from Roland Juno factory patches as decoded by Ultramaster KR-106 (GPL). 21 B-MINI and BITTAL sounds come from community Vital presets under free licenses: they are downloaded at a pinned commit, converted to the Bubba Machine format (tools/gen_snaps.mjs, assets/presets/banco.json) and given our own name. Factory patterns

Patterns 1-3 are recreations, played by the stock machines (no audio sampled): Da Funk (Daft Punk), Confusion in its Pump Panel Reconstruction from Blade (New Order) and Enjoy the Silence (Depeche Mode). The songs belong to their authors; the patterns only take tempo, key, chords and published sequencer sheets:

  • Confusion: the 303 line from the sheet on djjondent.blogspot.com, with the accents proposed on the Adafruit forums.
  • Enjoy the Silence: the drum grid from drum-patterns.com and the chorus chords from Hooktheory.
  • Da Funk: there is no published sheet; the notes follow Attack Magazine and Reverb Machine, and the order is by ear.
